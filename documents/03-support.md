# Support handbook

Fictional business: Northstar Studio. Educational sample only.

## H1 — Support hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC. Support is closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

## H2 — Response target

The team aims to provide an initial response within one business day. This is a response target, not a guaranteed resolution time.

## H3 — Contact support

Send support questions to support@northstar.example and include the project reference. This is a fictional address and does not receive messages.
