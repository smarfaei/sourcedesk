# Billing & cancellation

Fictional business: Northstar Studio. Educational sample only.

## B1 — Project payments

One-time projects require a 50% initial payment before work begins. The remaining 50% is due after approval of the agreed deliverables.

## B2 — Payment methods

Invoices can be paid by bank transfer. Card payments are not available in this fictional example.

## B3 — Subscription cancellation

Monthly maintenance subscriptions can be cancelled with 14 calendar days of notice before the next renewal date. Service continues until the end of the paid period.

## B4 — Refund policy

Completed work is non-refundable. For cancelled projects, unused prepaid work is reviewed against the approved proposal before a refund decision is made. Refunds are not automatic.
