# Services guide

Fictional business: Northstar Studio. Educational sample only.

## S1 — Available services

Northstar Studio offers website maintenance, workflow automation setup and monthly performance reporting.

## S2 — Getting started

To get started, submit a project brief through the contact form. The team reviews the brief, schedules a discovery call and sends a written proposal. Work starts after proposal approval and receipt of the initial payment.

## S3 — Scope and timing

Delivery dates and included tasks are defined in the approved proposal. Changes outside the agreed scope require a separate estimate and approval.
