# Learning log

## 2026-09-16 — Initial baseline

**Goal:** Explore document-grounded support as a step toward AI engineering and business automation.

**Starting point:** A prototype prepared with Codex assistance, using three fictional business documents, lexical retrieval, source references and an insufficient-information fallback.

**Initial validation:** Ten selected logic checks passed. Browser rendering and mobile interaction remain unverified. These checks are not a general accuracy benchmark.

**Observation from the recorded checks:** The question “Can I pay by card?” retrieves the payment-method section but also a project-payment section. Returning source text avoids generated prose but does not solve relevance.

**Next hands-on task:** Run the demo in a real browser, inspect each cited section, and record at least five additional questions, including a paraphrase and an unanswerable question.
